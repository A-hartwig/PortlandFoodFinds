## Portland, Oregon — 30 Lunch Specials Under $15

A deeply researched list of 30 Portland, Oregon restaurants and food carts with lunch specials or affordable lunch options under $15. Each entry links directly to the restaurant's menu or ordering page so you can verify prices yourself.

**Two categories are used:**
- 🎯 **Dedicated Lunch Special** — an explicitly named, time-limited deal available only at lunch (often includes extra items or a reduced price vs. dinner)
- 🍽️ **Affordable Lunch** — signature menu items priced under $15 available during lunch hours

> Prices are approximate and subject to change. Click each link to verify current pricing before you go.

### 1. Ha VL Vietnamese Noodle Soup 🎯
**Cuisine:** Vietnamese  
**Address:** 2738 SE 82nd Ave, Portland, OR 97266  
**Lunch Special:** Rotating **daily noodle soup specials** — each day (Wed–Sun) a different specialty soup is featured (e.g., Bún Bò Huế, Phở Gà, Hủ Tiếu Nam Vang, Bún Thang). A bowl is **~$9.50**. Arrive early — they often sell out. Cash only.  
**Hours:** Wed–Sun 8 AM–3 PM (sells out before close)  
**Menu/Verify Prices:** [Checkle menu listing](https://www.checkle.com/biz/ha-vl-vietnamese-noodle-soup-portland/menu) · [Daily soup rotation calendar](https://mrgan.com/havl/)
