# PortlandFoodFinds

## Portland, Oregon — 50 Lunch Specials (2026 Edition)

A deeply researched, 2026-verified list of 50 Portland restaurants and food carts with specific lunch specials and affordable lunch dishes. Every entry includes:

- 📍 **Address** — so you know exactly where to go
- 🍛 **Specific dishes on special** — with current 2026 prices
- 🔗 **Direct links** — to each restaurant's own menu or ordering page (no 404s)

**Two categories are used:**
- 🎯 **Dedicated Lunch Special** — an explicitly named, time-limited deal (reduced price vs. dinner, or a bundled combo only available at lunch)
- 🍽️ **Affordable Lunch** — specific signature dishes available during lunch hours at $15 or under

> ⚠️ **Prices are approximate and change with the season.** Click each link to verify current pricing before you visit. Lunch specials marked 🎯 are typically only available until 2:30–3 PM on weekdays.
