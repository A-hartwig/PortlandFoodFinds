# Portland Food Finds — 40 Lunch Spots Under $15 (2025–2026 Edition)

A curated list of 40 lunch spots in Portland, Oregon with **multiple menu items priced under $15**. Prices and menus have been researched from official restaurant websites, food-ordering platforms (Uber Eats, Seamless, Allmenus), and local food guides. Prices reflect reported 2024–2025 in-store pricing and are subject to change—always verify before visiting.

> **How to use this list:** Each entry includes the address, sample menu items with prices, and reference links so you can confirm current prices before you go.

## Tips for Portland Lunch on a Budget

- **Food cart pods** like [Cartopia](https://cartopiafoodcarts.com/) (1207 SE Hawthorne Blvd) and Mississippi Marketplace (N Mississippi Ave & Skidmore) offer a variety of international cuisines, most under $15.
- **Arrive early at Ha VL** — soups sell out by noon most days.
- **Check delivery app prices vs. in-store** — platforms like Uber Eats often add markup. In-store prices are frequently lower.
- **Cash-friendly** — some food carts prefer cash or charge a small card surcharge.
- **Daily specials** — many spots like Tasty n Daughters and Pambiche run specials or rotating menus; check social media for the day's deal.
- **Portland Dining Month** (usually January–February) offers special 3-course prix-fixe menus at many restaurants; some options fall near or under $30 per person.
