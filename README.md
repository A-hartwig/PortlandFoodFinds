# PortlandFoodFinds

## Portland, Oregon — Lunch Specials Under $15

A curated list of ~20 Portland restaurants and food carts offering lunch specials or menu items under $15. Prices are approximate and may change — always check with the restaurant before visiting.

> **Sources:** [Portland Monthly – Budget-Friendly Portland Dishes](https://www.pdxmonthly.com/eat-and-drink/best-cheap-eats-portland-budget-friendly-food) · [PDXtoday – Lunch Deals](https://pdxtoday.6amcity.com/city-guide/live/lunch-deals-portland-or) · [Travel Portland – Cheap Eats](https://www.travelportland.com/culture/cheap-eats-in-portland/) · [Eating the Globe – Best Cheap Eats Portland](https://eatingtheglobe.com/best-cheap-eats-portland-oregon/)

### 1. An Xuyen Bakery
**Cuisine:** Vietnamese  
**Special:** Fresh-baked baguette banh mi sandwiches (BBQ pork, lemongrass chicken, tofu, pâté, and more) — **from $6.75**  
**Menu/Info:** [Yelp listing](https://www.yelp.com/biz/an-xuyen-bakery-portland)

---

### 2. Basilisk
**Cuisine:** American (food hall)  
**Special:** Legendary fried chicken sandwich — **$13**; tofu sandwich — **$12**. Located in The Zipper food hall in the Kerns neighborhood.  
**Menu/Info:** [basiliskpdx.com/menu](https://www.basiliskpdx.com/menu)

---

### 3. Bing Mi
**Cuisine:** Chinese street food  
**Special:** Jian bing (savory Chinese crêpe with egg, crispy wonton, scallions, and sauce) — **from $10**  
**Menu/Info:** [bingmipdx.com/menu](https://www.bingmipdx.com/menu)

---

### 4. Bra Hund
**Cuisine:** Scandinavian  
**Special:** Scandinavian-style hot dogs — try the Norwegian (beef frank, mashed potatoes, fried onions, remoulade on lavash) — **$9**. Walk-up window on N. Mississippi Ave.  
**Menu/Info:** [PDXtoday listing](https://pdxtoday.6amcity.com/city-guide/live/lunch-deals-portland-or)

---

### 5. 808 Grinds
**Cuisine:** Hawaiian plate lunch  
**Special:** Shoyu chicken + kalua pork combo plate with rice and salad — **~$13**  
**Menu/Info:** [808grinds.com](https://www.808grinds.com/menu)

### 6. Tamale Boy
**Cuisine:** Mexican/Oaxacan
