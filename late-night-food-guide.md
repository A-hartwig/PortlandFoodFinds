# Portland Late Night & Early Morning Food Guide
### For Film Production Crews | Second Meal Edition

This guide is built for film and television production crews needing a **second meal** late at night or in the early morning hours in Portland, Oregon. Options are curated with dietary diversity (meat, vegetarian, and vegan) in mind, and all entries have been selected for their ability to handle large orders.

> ⚠️ **Important:** Restaurant hours change frequently. Always confirm directly with the restaurant before heading out. For orders of **50+ items**, call at least **1–2 hours ahead** (or the day before for very large orders).

## 🎬 Film Production Tips

### Ordering for 50+ People
- **Call ahead**: Always notify the restaurant 1–2 hours before pickup (ideally the day before for very large orders). This is the single most important step.
- **Ask for a group/catering point of contact**: Many establishments have a manager or catering coordinator who handles large orders more efficiently.
- **Stagger orders**: For diverse dietary needs, consider ordering from two nearby locations simultaneously (e.g., a vegan pizza from Sizzle Pie + a large diner order from Hotcake House).

### Best Picks by Category
- 🌙 **Best true 4 AM with full vegan options**: Sunee Thai & Lao Kitchen, Luc Lac Vietnamese Kitchen, Sizzle Pie
- 🌙 **Best 4 AM for bulk/chain reliability**: Denny's, Shari's, Taco Bell
- 🌅 **Best 7 AM for full catering service**: Cheryl's on 12th
- 🌅 **Best 7 AM for mixed-diet crew variety**: Pine State Biscuits, Cup and Saucer Cafe
- 🌅 **Best 7 AM for speed and volume**: McDonald's, Bernstein's Bagels, Pig 'N Pancake

### Delivery Options
After 2 AM, many Portland restaurants shift to delivery-only or reduce dine-in capacity. Services like **DoorDash**, **Uber Eats**, and **Grubhub** can expand your options — many 24-hour and late-night restaurants participate and can coordinate large delivery orders.
